# WinMedic v0.9.0

A tidier chart. Findings you have seen often enough can be archived now,
"Restart now" restarts again, and the update and print queue repairs no longer
stall on the services that depend on theirs. A restart is no longer counted as
a repair, and WinMedic is licensed under the GPL-3.0 from this release on.

---

## New

- **Archive findings:** a finding you do not want to see again, such as a
  device nobody uses or a known leftover, can be archived from its details in
  Advanced mode. It leaves the list, the health score, Easy mode and every
  repair run; Settings lists it with *Show again*. The command line leaves it
  out too, and the reports say how many findings are archived.

## Fixed

- **"Restart now" did nothing** in a WinMedic started by double-click. The
  window now closes only once Windows has accepted the restart. Opening the
  release page and starting a downloaded update could fail the same way.
- **The update cache and print queue repairs** failed while a service that
  depends on one they stop was running, such as AppLocker's Application
  Identity or Fax: `net stop` asked first and, with nobody to answer, stopped
  nothing. Those services are now stopped first and started again afterwards.
- **Recent activity** left out entries written at the same moment as another
  one. Older logs are read in full.
- The HTML report's first card says "Findings" instead of "Gesamte Befunde".

## Repairs that are honest now

- **A restart is not a repair:** a restart Windows has queued for its updates,
  and a restart that is overdue while Fast Startup is off, are advice now
  instead of repairs that changed nothing. The banner no longer says "Restart
  Windows to finish 1 repair." when there is none, and the restart dialog says
  what the restart finishes. A scan saved by 0.8.0 is read the new way.

## License

WinMedic moves from MIT to the **GPL-3.0**: it stays free and open source, and
nobody may sell a closed version of it. 0.8.0 and earlier stay under MIT.
