# WinMedic v0.16.0

In motion. The window no longer jumps from one state to the next: pages,
dialogs, banners and progress bars move the way Windows 11's own do, and
none of it makes you wait.

---

## New

- **Pages** come up into their place and fade in: when you pick another view
  or switch between Easy and Advanced mode, and when a view has something
  else to say, such as scanning, repairing or the result.
- **The navigation's accent bar** glides to the page you pick.
- **Findings:** a checkbox fades its fill in and draws its tick. A row fades
  its highlight in under the pointer and when it is selected.
- **Dialogs** settle into their size, the log slides open and shut, and the
  update, restart and crash banners slide in under the navigation instead
  of throwing the page down.
- **The progress bars** of a scan and a repair run to their value instead of
  moving in the steps the scan reports.

## Good to know

- Every animation ends after a quarter of a second at the latest, and the
  window is then as still as before. While a scan or repair runs it is
  redrawn no more often than in 0.15.0.
- With "Animation effects" switched off in Windows (Settings ->
  Accessibility -> Visual effects; "Show animations in Windows" on Windows
  10), nothing animates. WinMedic reads the setting when its window opens.
