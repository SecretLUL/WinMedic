# WinMedic v0.17.0

A clean exit. WinMedic now removes itself from a PC in one step, with
everything it keeps there, and an update shows how far its download is
instead of a spinner.

---

## New

- **Remove WinMedic from this PC:** *Settings -> Remove WinMedic from this
  PC* asks once, naming what goes and where it is. Then the window closes
  and WinMedic deletes the background scan task, your settings, logs and
  reports, the registry backups, WinGet's entry if WinGet installed it, and
  `winmedic.exe` itself. Nothing is left to do by hand. Until now the button
  removed only the background scan; the rest took two commands and a file to
  delete.
- **An update shows how far it is:** after *Download and restart* the banner
  said "Updating..." beside a spinner for as long as the download took. It
  now says how many megabytes have arrived, out of how many, with a bar, and
  then names each step: the checksum, the signature, the installation. The
  taskbar button fills with the download, as it does with a scan.

## Good to know

- **What the removal takes with it:** without the registry backups, earlier
  repairs can no longer be undone from WinMedic. The dialog says so before
  you confirm. Windows' own restore points stay.
- **When it is offered:** not while a scan, a repair, a rollback or an update
  runs.
- **The program's folder** goes as well when it is one named `WinMedic`, such
  as `C:\Program Files\WinMedic`, with nothing else in it.
- **WinGet:** its entry is removed when WinMedic runs as the account that
  installed it. Under another account the entry stays.
- **Scripts:** `winmedic --uninstall` and `winmedic --uninstall --purge` work
  as before.
- **The update to 0.17.0 itself** still shows the old banner: it is 0.16.0
  that installs it. The new one appears from the update after this.
