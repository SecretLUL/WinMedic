# WinMedic v0.18.0

Knowing how long. Easy mode now says about how long a repair takes before
you start it, and tells you early that all is well while it runs.

---

## New

- **How long the repair takes:** under *What Repair does*, one line says it
  before you click *Repair*: "The repair takes about a minute.", "a few
  minutes" when the component store cleanup is among the repairs, and "can
  take 15 minutes or longer" when DISM, SFC or a disk check is. Until now the
  page said what a repair does and nothing about its length, and a bar that
  hardly moves for ten minutes came as a surprise.
- **Reassurance after half a minute:** "Don't worry, everything is fine.
  WinMedic needs some time to heal." used to appear once one repair step had
  run for two minutes. It now appears after 30 seconds, which is about when
  someone who has never watched DISM starts to wonder whether the window
  hangs. How long the step still takes follows at two minutes, as before.

## Good to know

- **The time is an estimate,** in three steps and without an upper end for
  the slow repairs: they take as long as the disk, the damage and the
  download allow. A simulation runs no repair and shows no time.
- **Advanced mode** is unchanged: it shows each step and how long it has
  been running, as before.
- **Chocolatey:** from this release on, each version is also submitted to
  the Chocolatey community repository once its VirusTotal scan is clean. A
  version appears there when Chocolatey's moderation has approved it.
- **Preview versions:** a version with `-pre` in its name, such as
  `v0.18.1-pre.1`, is for trying a change before it is released. WinMedic
  never offers one as an update, and WinGet and Chocolatey do not carry it:
  it is a download by hand from the releases page.
